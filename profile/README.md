<h1 align="center">All about cracker.rip</h1>

cracker.rip is a small self-hosted corner of the internet: chat, social media, code, search and game servers for a group of friends, running on our own hardware in the Netherlands. Calypso keeps an eye on it. Accounts are invite-only; see the terms for how to apply.
